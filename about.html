About Voltline

Wiring the projects that keep our region running.

Voltline Electric is a family-owned electrical contractor founded in 1984. Four decades in, we still run the company the way our founder did: hire well, train relentlessly, treat safety as a non-negotiable, and tell clients the truth about their schedule.

Our story

Started by an electrician. Still run like one.

We started with two trucks and a handshake reputation. Today we run multiple yards and a workforce of journeymen, apprentices, project managers, and estimators -- but every project still gets the same question we have asked for forty years: is this work we would put our name on?

Our growth has been deliberate. We say no to projects we cannot staff well. We promote from within. And we keep our work self-performed, because that is the only way to control the quality our clients pay for.

What we stand for

Three values, applied every day

SF

Safety first, always

OSHA 30 supervision on every site. Daily JHAs. A culture where any team member can stop work and nobody asks why. Our 0.62 EMR is not an accident -- it is a discipline.

CR

Craft over volume

We would rather do 80 projects right than 100 projects okay. Workmanship is what gets us repeat clients, and repeat clients are what keep our crews working in good times and bad.

TR

Truth on the schedule

If a date is not realistic, we say so before we sign. If a problem is brewing, you hear it from us first. Our clients trust us with their hardest projects because we do not surprise them.
