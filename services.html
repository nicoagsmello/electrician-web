What we do

Full-scope electrical contracting

Six core service lines, one accountable team. Whether you need preconstruction support on a $20M build or a same-day service truck, we run the same playbook: scope it honestly, schedule it realistically, and self-perform with crews who own the work.

Service 01

Commercial Construction

Tenant fit-outs, ground-up builds, retail rollouts, healthcare, and institutional work. We work directly with general contractors, owners, and developers from preconstruction through closeout, and we self-perform the install so quality and schedule never get diluted.

  • Office and tenant improvement
  • Retail rollouts and multi-site programs
  • Hospitality and healthcare
  • K-12, higher ed, and municipal
  • BIM coordination and prefabrication
Service 02

Industrial & MEP

Plant power, switchgear, motor controls, instrumentation, and conveyance. We support manufacturers, distribution centers, and process facilities where uptime is the metric and documentation matters as much as the install.

  • Medium-voltage distribution and switchgear
  • Motor control centers and VFDs
  • PLC integration and instrumentation
  • Conveyor and material-handling power
  • Arc flash studies and coordination
Service 03

Design-Build

Engineering and field execution under one contract. We bring our preconstruction team in early, optimize the system before steel goes up, and own both the design intent and the install. Owners get a single point of accountability and a faster path to occupancy.

  • In-house electrical engineering and stamping partners
  • Energy modeling and incentive coordination
  • Value engineering with real cost data
  • Single-contract delivery
Service 04

Service & Maintenance

Preventive maintenance contracts, code corrections, infrared scans, and 24/7 emergency response across single buildings or full portfolios. Our service trucks are dispatched from local yards so response times are measured in minutes, not hours.

  • Preventive maintenance agreements
  • Infrared thermography and PM testing
  • Code corrections and AHJ coordination
  • Tenant turnover and small projects
  • 24/7 emergency dispatch
Service 05

EV Charging Infrastructure

Site assessments, utility coordination, and turnkey installs for fleet depots, multi-family properties, workplace charging, and DCFC sites. We handle the make-ready scope, the network commissioning, and the rebate paperwork.

  • Level 2 and DCFC installations
  • Service upgrades and switchgear additions
  • Utility and AHJ coordination
  • Network commissioning and warranty
  • Rebate and incentive support
Service 06

Data & Low Voltage

Structured cabling, fiber backbones, access control, and AV infrastructure. Built to BICSI standards, tested with calibrated equipment, and documented so your IT team isn't guessing two years from now.

  • Cat6/6A structured cabling
  • Single-mode and multimode fiber
  • Access control and IP video
  • AV and conference room rough-in
  • BICSI-certified design and testing
